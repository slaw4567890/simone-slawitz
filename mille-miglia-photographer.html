Classic cars, Italian roads and the visual culture of heritage motoring — photographed with a quiet editorial approach.

01 · Mille Miglia Photographer

A race made of memory.

Mille Miglia is not only a classic car event. It is a moving archive of Italian roads, design, people, weather, waiting and mechanical presence. Simone Slawitz photographs this atmosphere with a reportage approach rooted in heritage.

The work connects classic cars, Italian automotive history, road culture, collectors, crews and public ritual, creating images that are useful for editorial stories, brand communication, event archives and luxury automotive storytelling.

Rather than focusing only on speed, the visual language follows the human and cultural dimension of the event: the gestures before departure, the details of the cars, the roadside spectators and the texture of Italy itself.
