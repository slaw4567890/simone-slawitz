01 · Historic Cars Photographer Italy

A language of heritage.

Historic cars are not only machines. They are objects of memory, design, craft and identity. Simone Slawitz photographs classic automotive culture with an editorial method that values atmosphere as much as movement.

The work is built around historic cars, heritage racing, classic car events, concours culture and automotive details: bodywork, hands, badges, interiors, light, waiting and the social rituals around the car.

This approach is designed for collectors, brands, magazines, museums, event organisers and luxury contexts that need images with restraint, precision and cultural depth.

Design, patina and presence.

From the intimacy of paddocks to Italian roads and concours lawns, the photographic narrative moves between documentary observation and refined editorial composition. The aim is not to make the car louder, but to reveal its presence.
