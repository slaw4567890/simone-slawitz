Behind the Visor

2026
Monaco · Imola

A series of portraits made through the opening of the helmet, mostly at 400 mm and from a distance, so as not to interrupt the moment. Before the start the driver disappears into the helmet: only the eyes remain.

Wrinkles, concentration, a smile before the green flag: the people who make historic racing, photographed in the seconds the camera usually misses. Available as a private commission for drivers and owners during a race weekend.

Monaco Grand Prix Historique

2026
Circuit de Monaco · Monte Carlo

Biennial event by the Automobile Club de Monaco: since 1997 it has brought some of the world's rarest historic Formula 1 cars to the most iconic circuit — Sainte-Dévote, the Loews hairpin, the tunnel, the harbour.

Rituals on the grid, yellow flags, a damaged Tyrrell, cars lifted off the streets by crane: Formula 1 when it still smelled of petrol and hot oil, and the people who keep it alive.

The circuit was a tunnel for me, and I was just going and going and going ahead. — Ayrton Senna, Monaco 1988

Gran Premio Storico d'Italia

2026
Autodromo Internazionale del Mugello · Scarperia

The first edition, 2 — 4 October 2026: historic single-seaters from the 1950s to the 2000s back on track at Mugello, with a tribute to Ferrari's golden decade of 1996 — 2006.

My photographs of the weekend illustrated the Classic Driver feature on the event. Here, the side the grandstands do not see: light cutting across the garages, children at the pit lane, the circuit at dusk.

Chapter IV

Mille Miglia

2022 — 2025
Brescia · Parma · Roma — Italy

Called by Enzo Ferrari “the most beautiful race in the world”, the Mille Miglia embodies Italian motorsport as passion, history and art. Since 1927 it has run from Brescia to Rome and back, crossing towns, squares and lives.

This is where my work received two Bruno Boni Photo Contest honours: not only speed and courage, but the fervent emotion of the crowd around the race.

The Mille Miglia is unique in its kind, the most beautiful race in the world. — Enzo Ferrari

Concorso d'Eleganza Villa d'Este

2026
Villa d'Este · Villa Erba · Lake Como

One of the most refined stages for historic automobiles, where automotive heritage, design culture and the atmosphere of Lake Como meet in a discreet, highly curated setting.

The elegance of the cars, the gestures around them, the lakeside setting: an event where beauty is judged through proportion, history and presence.

Elegance is not standing out, but being remembered. — Giorgio Armani

Chapter VI

FIA WEC

2024 — 2026
6 Hours of Imola · 6 Hours of Monza

Endurance racing is more than a test of speed: it is coordination, adaptability and resilience. Imola, with its history and technical corners, is where precision driving becomes essential.

At Imola I documented Ferrari's rebirth with the 499P: corner jumps, podium crowds, flags and fragments of a race where every second carries a story. In 2026 I also covered the season finale at Monza, published by Fuori Traiettoria. The set mixes both sides of the job: authored frames — panning, sparks, rain — and clean editorial images of cars, crews and pit stops that teams, sponsors and media can use straight away.

If everything seems under control, you're just not going fast enough. — Mario Andretti
