Monaco Grand Prix Historique

2026
Circuit de Monaco · Monte Carlo

Biennial event by the Automobile Club de Monaco: since 1997 it has brought some of the world's rarest historic Formula 1 cars to the most iconic circuit — Sainte-Dévote, the Loews hairpin, the tunnel, the harbour.

Pitlane warm-ups, cars on the grid, the Ferrari parade, Monegasque flags around the Casino: Formula 1 when it still smelled of petrol and hot oil.

The circuit was a tunnel for me, and I was just going and going and going ahead. — Ayrton Senna, Monaco 1988

Mille Miglia

2022 — 2025
Brescia · Parma · Roma — Italy

Called by Enzo Ferrari “the most beautiful race in the world”, the Mille Miglia embodies Italian motorsport as passion, history and art. Since 1927 it has run from Brescia to Rome and back, crossing towns, squares and lives.

This is where my work received two Bruno Boni Photo Contest awards: not only speed and courage, but the fervent emotion of the crowd around the race.

The Mille Miglia is unique in its kind, the most beautiful race in the world. — Enzo Ferrari

Concorso d'Eleganza Villa d'Este

2026
Villa d'Este · Villa Erba · Lake Como

One of the most refined stages for historic automobiles, where automotive heritage, design culture and the atmosphere of Lake Como meet in a discreet, highly curated setting.

The elegance of the cars, the gestures around them, the lakeside setting: an event where beauty is judged through proportion, history and presence.

Elegance is not standing out, but being remembered. — Giorgio Armani

WEC 6H Imola

2024 — 2026
Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari · Imola

Endurance racing is more than a test of speed: it is coordination, adaptability and resilience. Imola, with its history and technical corners, is where precision driving becomes essential.

Here I documented Ferrari's rebirth with the 499P: corner jumps, podium crowds, flags and fragments of a race where every second carries a story.

If everything seems under control, you're just not going fast enough. — Mario Andretti
