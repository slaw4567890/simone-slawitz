Editorial photography for historic cars, luxury automotive culture, Lake Como atmosphere and the refined rituals of concours d’elegance.

01 · Concorso d’Eleganza Villa d’Este Photographer

Where design becomes atmosphere.

Villa d’Este is a natural meeting point between automotive design, history, landscape and discreet luxury. Simone Slawitz approaches this world through editorial reportage: not only the car, but the gestures, setting, light and attention around it.

The visual direction is suited to Concorso d’Eleganza, historic cars, luxury automotive events, hospitality, watchmaking and heritage brands that need a sophisticated visual language rather than conventional event coverage.

The goal is to create images that feel quiet, precise and culturally positioned: photographs able to speak to collectors, brands, magazines and institutions connected to automotive elegance.

02 · Lake Como & Concours Culture

Cars, people and place.

The strength of a concours lies in the relationship between object and setting: historic cars, architecture, hospitality, design, territory and the culture of collecting. The photographic approach follows that relationship with restraint and a heritage-oriented eye.
